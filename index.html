Impact Einsteins study of Brownian motion led to the discovery of atoms. Brownian motion is the movement of particles in liquid and gas, for example, pollen in water. The molecules in the water are constantly moving around randomly, so when you drop pollen in, the molecules hit the pollen and make it move, which is why it has a zig-zag motion. Einstein also used James Clerk Maxwell's equations describing light and Hendrik Lorentz's equations on moving objects to create the Theory of Relativity. The Special Theory of Relativity says that the speed of a moving object depends on how it's being viewed. From this, he also came up with the equation E=mc^2, which means energy is equal to mass times the speed of light multiplied by itself.
